How it works

Your car.Your call.

Crash keeps the same four cars and asks a different question. Not who wins, but how long you dare to stay in.

  1. Choose your car

    Pick one of the four cars before the round. Each car has its own crash point, separate from the others.

  2. Watch the multiplier rise

    Once the race is running the multiplier climbs. Where a car is on the circuit is part of the show. It does not tell you when that car will crash.

  3. Cash out in time

    Cash out while your car is still running to lock in the multiplier. If your car crashes first, the round is lost.

This describes the game concept. Prototypes use simulated funds, and features and rules may change during development.

Features

Made forthe decision.

  • Individual crash outcomes

    Cars drop out one at a time, each on its own. There is no shared finish line.

  • Multiplier led format

    What you cash out is your stake times the multiplier at the moment you leave.

  • One decision per round

    There is nothing to manage except timing, which keeps the game easy to explain.

  • The same NightGrid world

    The four cars and the night circuit carry over, so both games feel like one collection.

  • A standalone title

    Crash is developed as its own game alongside Grand Prix, with its own rules and rhythm.

  • Simulated funds

    Every balance and result in the prototype is demo money with no cash value.
