How it works

Pick a car.Watch it race.

Grand Prix is the simplest idea in racing. Whoever crosses the line first, wins.

  1. Choose your car

    Blue, Red, Black and Purple line up on the grid. Pick the one you think will win before the race starts.

  2. Follow the race

    The four cars run the circuit lap by lap. Positions change, and the tension builds as they come back to the start and finish line.

  3. First place decides it

    Only the winner counts. If your car finishes first, your pick wins. Second, third and fourth place do not pay.

This describes the game concept. Prototypes use simulated funds, and features and rules may change during development.

Features

Made forthe finish line.

  • Four car identity

    Blue, Red, Black and Purple make the grid recognisable at a glance, round after round.

  • One clear result

    A winner based format. There is no cash out to manage, only the question of who finishes first.

  • A full racing world

    The circuit, the kerbs, the marshals and the chequered flag give the game a setting of its own.

  • Readable at any size

    Car positions, laps and results are designed to be easy to follow on a phone or a desktop.

  • Runs in the browser

    The public prototype opens in a new tab with nothing to install.

  • Simulated funds

    Every balance and result in the prototype is demo money with no cash value.
