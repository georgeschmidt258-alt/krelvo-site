About this website

This website showcases the KRELVO studio and its NightGrid game concepts. It is not a real-money gambling service, and it does not accept deposits or pay winnings.

Development prototypes

Linked game prototypes use simulated funds with no monetary value. Features and presentation can change during development. Nothing on this website represents a claim of regulatory approval, independent certification, or a commercial operator partnership.

Privacy and external services

This static showcase has no visitor accounts, advertising trackers or analytics scripts added by KRELVO. Fonts and scripts are served from this site. The partner enquiry form sends only the details you type into it, either through your own email app or to the studio’s enquiry inbox. Hosting services may process technical information needed to serve and secure the website. Linked game sites have their own hosting and access controls.

Artwork and content

Animated track previews use artwork and visual code from Grand Prix v57 and Crash v42. They run independent local showcase rounds, not shared game sessions. Crash timings and displayed multipliers in the preview are illustrative. The original games may be updated separately. GRID CHICKEN screens and the gameplay clip are captured from the public demo. The showroom and night circuit backdrops, the 3D car model and the 3D chicken on this site are atmospheric artwork made for the website. They are not game screens.
