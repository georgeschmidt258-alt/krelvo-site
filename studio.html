KRELVO is an independent game studio. We design and build original racing games for iGaming, starting with the NightGrid collection and GRID CHICKEN.

Our story

KRELVO was founded in 2026 by George Schmidt. He set out to build original racing games for iGaming. The first result is NightGrid: four cars, one night circuit and two very different games. GRID CHICKEN followed, with the same four cars and a very different hero. All three exist as prototypes today. We say plainly what is ready and what is still being built.

Values and approach

Three thingswe hold to.

They shape what we build, how we present it, and how we talk to the people who might one day run it.

01

Original IP

NightGrid is ours. The cars, the circuit, the formats and the game maths are designed in the studio, so the collection looks and plays like nothing else on a lobby page.

02

Transparent play

Rules should fit in a sentence and results should be easy to read. Our prototypes use simulated funds, and we label them for what they are.

03

Operator focus

We build for the teams who will evaluate, integrate and run these games. That means clear information, realistic status and no surprises.
