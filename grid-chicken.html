How it works

Cross a lane.Or collect.

Every lane you cross is worth more than the last. The only decision is whether to take one more step.

  1. Set your run

    Choose a demo bet and a difficulty. Easy has 15 lanes, Medium has 20 and Hard has 25. Harder modes climb faster and carry more risk.

  2. Cross one lane at a time

    BET starts the run and crosses the first lane. NEXT crosses one more. Each safe crossing raises the amount you can collect.

  3. Collect before a collision

    Cash out between crossings to bank your return. A collision ends the run and that run’s bet is lost. Reach the finish and it collects for you.

Standing still is safe. The passing traffic is part of the show, and timing your click does not change the odds. This describes the current prototype, which uses demo funds only. Features and rules may change during development.

Features

Made forone more lane.

  • Three difficulty levels

    Easy, Medium and Hard change the number of lanes and how quickly the multiplier climbs.

  • Collect any time between crossings

    The cash out button always shows exactly what you would bank.

  • Auto Play

    Set a number of runs and the lane to collect at, and stop whenever you like.

  • The same four cars

    Blue, Red, Black and Purple are the traffic, straight from the NightGrid grid.

  • Built for phones and keyboards

    One thumb is enough on a phone. On desktop, Space or the right arrow crosses and C collects.

  • Demo funds only

    Every balance and result in the prototype is demo money with no cash value, saved in your browser.
